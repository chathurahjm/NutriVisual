## Sarcopenia and Aging

Sarcopenia—the age-related loss of muscle mass and strength—is one of the primary drivers of metabolic decline and fragility in later life. To prevent this, physical training must be paired with adequate protein intake.

However, simply eating protein is not enough; you must consume enough of the amino acid Leucine to turn on the cellular pathway responsible for building muscle (mTORC1).

## The Leucine Threshold

Research shows you need roughly 2.5 to 3 grams of Leucine per meal to trigger muscle protein synthesis. This is known as the 'leucine trigger.'

Whey protein, Greek Yogurt, Eggs, and Chicken Breast are highly bioavailable sources rich in leucine. For plant-based diets, tofu and tempeh must be consumed in larger portions or combined with other amino acids to hit the same trigger.
