## Sarcopenia and Metabolic Decline

As we age, we naturally lose muscle mass and strength—a process known as **sarcopenia**. Because muscle is your body's largest glucose sink, losing muscle directly accelerates insulin resistance and metabolic decline.

To maintain muscle, you must trigger the **mTORC1 pathway**, the master cellular regulator of protein synthesis. This pathway is specifically activated by the essential amino acid **Leucine**.

---

## Hitting the Leucine Trigger

To turn on muscle protein synthesis, you must hit the **"leucine trigger"** of roughly **2.5 to 3 grams** of leucine in a single meal.

| Food | Protein Per 100g | Leucine Per 100g | Serving to Hit Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Greek Yogurt** | ~10g | ~1.0g | ~250g |
| **Firm Tofu** | ~8g | ~0.6g | ~450g |
| **Chicken Breast** | ~31g | ~2.5g | ~110g |

Animal-derived proteins like Greek yogurt contain a higher concentration of leucine, requiring smaller portion volumes. Plant-based sources like tofu are highly valuable but must be consumed in larger portions or combined with other proteins to reach the leucine trigger.
