## Activating the Nrf2 Pathway

Our bodies have an built-in antioxidant response element called the Nrf2 pathway. It controls hundreds of genes related to detoxification and cellular cleanup.

Compounds like Sulforaphane (found in broccoli and cruciferous vegetables) and Allicin (found in fresh garlic) are powerful activators of this pathway, helping defend your DNA from environmental pollutants and cellular aging.
