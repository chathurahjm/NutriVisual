## Is Avocado Healthy?

**Yes — avocado is a nutrient-dense fruit loaded with monounsaturated fat, fiber, and potassium.** However, at **160 calories per 100g** (and ~320 calories per whole avocado), it's also one of the most calorie-dense fruits on the planet, making portion awareness essential.

## Why Avocado Is Healthy

### 1. Heart-Healthy Monounsaturated Fats
Avocado's fat profile is dominated by **oleic acid** (the same fat in olive oil), which has been shown to:
- Reduce LDL ("bad") cholesterol
- Increase HDL ("good") cholesterol
- Lower systemic inflammation markers

### 2. Exceptional Fiber Content
At **6.7g of fiber per 100g**, avocado is one of the highest-fiber fruits. This soluble fiber feeds beneficial gut bacteria and slows glucose absorption, preventing blood sugar spikes.

### 3. Potassium Powerhouse
Avocados contain **more potassium per serving than bananas** (485mg vs 358mg per 100g), supporting blood pressure regulation and neuromuscular function.

## The Avocado Calorie Trap

Despite being healthy, avocado is a common source of **hidden calorie surplus**. Here's the math:

| Portion | Weight | Calories |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 whole medium avocado | ~200g | **320 kcal** |
| Half an avocado | ~100g | 160 kcal |
| Avocado toast (with 2 tbsp smashed) | ~50g avocado + bread | ~250 kcal |
| Restaurant guacamole (1 serving) | ~150g | 240+ kcal |

Adding a whole avocado to a meal adds the caloric equivalent of a full chicken breast — but without the 31g of protein.

## How Much Avocado Should You Eat Per Day?

For most adults, **one-third to one-half of a medium avocado per day** (50–100g, or 80–160 kcal) is a balanced amount that delivers the health benefits without significantly impacting total calorie intake.

If you're physically active and not tracking calories, a full avocado daily is perfectly fine — just account for it in your overall fat and calorie budget.
