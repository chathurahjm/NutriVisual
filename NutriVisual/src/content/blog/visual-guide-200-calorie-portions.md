Calorie density is the single biggest factor governing hunger during weight management. Two hundred calories of raw spinach fills a large salad bowl, while two hundred calories of extra virgin olive oil fits inside less than two tablespoons.

Understanding **volume eating**—selecting foods with low caloric density and high water/fiber content—allows you to consume larger physical meals while maintaining a caloric deficit.

Here is what **200 calories** looks like across six distinct food categories.

---

## 1. Fresh Broccoli (Low Calorie Density)
- **Portion Weight:** ~588 grams (1.3 lbs)
- **Visual Scale:** 3 whole heads of broccoli filling a large mixing bowl.
- **Nutritional Profile:** 200 kcal | 16.5g Protein | 41g Carbs | 15g Fiber

*Volume Impact:* Extremely high. The high water and insoluble fiber structure stretches stomach wall mechanoreceptors (vagal afferent stretch signals), triggering ghrelin suppression.

---

## 2. Whole Avocado (Moderate Fat Density)
- **Portion Weight:** ~125 grams (~0.75 medium avocado)
- **Visual Scale:** Three-quarters of a medium avocado sliced on a small plate.
- **Nutritional Profile:** 200 kcal | 2.5g Protein | 18.5g Fat | 8.5g Fiber

*Volume Impact:* Moderate. High monounsaturated fat provides sustained hormonal satiety (via CCK release) despite smaller physical volume than vegetables.

---

## 3. Extra Virgin Olive Oil (Ultra High Calorie Density)
- **Portion Weight:** ~22.5 grams (~1.6 tablespoons)
- **Visual Scale:** Less than two tablespoons sitting at the bottom of a small ramekin.
- **Nutritional Profile:** 200 kcal | 0g Protein | 22.5g Fat | 0g Fiber

*Volume Impact:* Very low. Pure fat liquid contains no water or fiber. It provides no stomach stretch signals despite delivering 200 energy units instantly.

---

## 4. Whole Raw Almonds (High Density)
- **Portion Weight:** ~35 grams (~28-30 individual almonds)
- **Visual Scale:** A small single handful.
- **Nutritional Profile:** 200 kcal | 7.4g Protein | 17.5g Fat | 4.4g Fiber

*Volume Impact:* Low-Moderate. Highly nutritious, but easy to overconsume because a small visual portion packs high caloric energy.

---

## 5. Boiled White Potato (High Satiety Starch)
- **Portion Weight:** ~230 grams (~1.5 medium potatoes)
- **Visual Scale:** One and a half medium boiled potatoes on a plate.
- **Nutritional Profile:** 200 kcal | 4.4g Protein | 46g Carbs | 4.1g Fiber

*Volume Impact:* High. Boiled potatoes rank #1 on the official **Holt Satiety Index** (scoring 323% relative to white bread at 100%).

## Takeaway for Smart Swapping

To maximize fullness on lower calories, build 70% of your plate around **low-density volume foods** (cruciferous greens, tubers, berries) and use **high-density fats** (oils, butter, nuts) mindfully as flavor accents rather than bulk ingredients.
