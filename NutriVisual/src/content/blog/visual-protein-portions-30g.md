## The 30g Protein Target for Muscle Protein Synthesis

Nutritional science demonstrates that hitting a minimum threshold of **30 grams of high-quality protein per meal** is the optimal target to trigger **Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS)** via the mTOR pathway in adults.

However, estimating what 30 grams of protein actually looks like on a plate can be challenging without carrying a digital food scale. Below is a spatial and volumetric guide to hitting 30g of protein across common whole foods.

---

## Protein Efficiency: Protein-to-Calorie Ratio

When selecting protein sources for weight management or lean muscle building, the **protein-to-calorie ratio** is a critical metric:

> **Protein Ratio = (Protein Grams × 4 kcal) / Total Calories**

```
[ Ultra Lean (80-90% Protein Ratio) ]
Skinless Chicken Breast, Cod Fillet, Egg Whites, Whey Isolate

[ Moderate Lean (50-70% Protein Ratio) ]
Lean Sirloin Steak, Atlantic Salmon, Cottage Cheese, Tofu

[ Calorie Dense (20-40% Protein Ratio) ]
Whole Eggs, Whole Almonds, Peanut Butter, Black Beans
```

---

## 3 Rules for Hitting Your Protein Target

1. **Prioritize Single-Ingredient Anchor Proteins**: Build your meal around a primary protein source (chicken, sirloin, salmon, or eggs) before adding carbs or fats.
2. **Combine Plant Proteins**: Because plant sources like lentils or quinoa have lower protein density per gram, pair them with tofu or soy isolates to hit 30g without excess carbs.
3. **Use the Visual Palm Marker**: One thick palm-sized piece of cooked poultry or meat delivers right around 30g of complete protein.
