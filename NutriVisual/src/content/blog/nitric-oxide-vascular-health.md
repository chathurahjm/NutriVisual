## What is Nitric Oxide?

Nitric oxide is a gas produced naturally in the body that signals blood vessels to relax, dilate, and expand. This process, known as vasodilation, is essential for maintaining optimal blood flow, reducing blood pressure, and delivering oxygen to active tissues.

As we age, our natural production of nitric oxide drops significantly, leading to arterial stiffness and cardiovascular risk.

## Boosting Nitric Oxide with Food

You can increase your nitric oxide levels by eating foods rich in inorganic nitrates. In your mouth, symbiotic bacteria convert these nitrates into nitrites, which are then converted to nitric oxide in your stomach.

Red Beets, Spinach, Pomegranate, and dark leafy greens are excellent nitrate boosters. Eating them raw or lightly steamed preserves these compounds for maximum cardiovascular support.
