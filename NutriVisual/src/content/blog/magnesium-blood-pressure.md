## The Silent Mineral Deficiency

Over 50% of the population is deficient in magnesium. This mineral acts as a natural calcium channel blocker, allowing blood vessels to relax and dilate, which naturally lowers blood pressure.

Instead of relying on synthetic supplements, loading your diet with whole-food magnesium is highly bioavailable and easily absorbed.

## The Top Dietary Sources

Pumpkin seeds are a magnesium goldmine, providing over 150mg of magnesium per ounce. Dark leafy greens like Spinach and Swiss Chard are also packed with this critical mineral, alongside raw cacao nibs and almonds.
