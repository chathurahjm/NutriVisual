## The Engine of Your Cells

Every single cell in your body contains thousands of microscopic engines called sodium-potassium pumps. This pump actively moves sodium out of the cell and pulls potassium in, creating an electrical gradient.

This gradient is responsible for nerve transmission, muscle contraction, and cellular hydration. Without sufficient potassium, your cells accumulate excess sodium, leading to intracellular bloating, chronic fatigue, and elevated blood pressure.

## The Modern Dietary Imbalance

Our ancestors consumed roughly 4 times more potassium than sodium. Today, the modern diet has flipped this ratio, containing up to 3 times more sodium than potassium.

To restore metabolic harmony, you should focus on adding potassium-rich whole foods like Avocado and Spinach to buffer the sodium content in your diet and relax blood vessels.
