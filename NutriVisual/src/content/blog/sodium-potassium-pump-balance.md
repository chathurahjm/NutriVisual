## The Electrical Gradient of Life

Every cell in your body runs on electricity. To generate this energy, cell membranes contain thousands of active enzymes called **sodium-potassium pumps (Na<sup>+</sup>/K<sup>+</sup>-ATPase)**.

For every cycle, this pump moves **3 sodium ions out** of the cell and pulls **2 potassium ions in**. This uneven exchange creates an electrical gradient that controls:
1. Nerve impulse transmission.
2. Muscle contraction and recovery.
3. Intracellular hydration and fluid balance.

---

## Restoring the Ancestral Ratio

Modern diets contain up to 3 times more sodium than potassium, leading to cellular bloating and arterial stiffness.

| Mineral | Primary Location | Daily Goal | Whole Food Sources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sodium** | Extracellular | < 2,300 mg | Sea salt, fermented foods |
| **Potassium** | Intracellular | ~4,700 mg | Avocado, Spinach, Coconut water |

Increasing your potassium intake by eating half an avocado or a serving of spinach helps restore this ancestral mineral ratio, reducing fluid retention and lowering blood pressure.
