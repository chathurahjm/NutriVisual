## Cellular Bioenergetics of Electrolytes

Every cell in the human body operates as a battery powered by an electrochemical gradient across its plasma membrane. The master regulator of this vital cellular charge is the **Sodium-Potassium Pump (Na+/K+-ATPase)**.

This membrane-bound enzyme accounts for over 20% to 30% of total resting energy expenditure (ATP consumption) in the human body, maintaining low intracellular sodium ($Na^+$) and high intracellular potassium ($K^+$) concentrations.

---

## How the Na+/K+-ATPase Pump Works

For every molecule of ATP hydrolyzed, the sodium-potassium pump undergoes a conformational cycle:
1. Expels **3 Sodium ions ($Na^+$)** out of the cell into extracellular fluid.
2. Imports **2 Potassium ions ($K^+$)** into the cell cytoplasm.

This net movement of 3 positive charges out for every 2 positive charges in creates a negative resting membrane potential (~ -70 mV), which powers:
* **Neuronal Action Potentials:** Nerve impulse transmission in the brain and peripheral nerves.
* **Cardiomyocyte Contraction:** Rhythmic heart contraction and vascular tone regulation.
* **Secondary Active Transport:** Intestinal absorption of glucose and amino acids.

---

## The Dietary K:Na Ratio & Cardiovascular Health

In ancestral human diets, the ratio of dietary potassium to sodium ($K:Na$) was approximately **4:1 to 10:1** (high plant intake, low processed salt). In modern processed diets, this ratio has inverted to approximately **1:3** (low plant intake, excessive added sodium).

### Clinical Consequences of an Inverted K:Na Ratio
Excess sodium without adequate potassium increases extracellular fluid volume, placing hydrostatic pressure on arterial walls. Simultaneously, low potassium downregulates sodium-potassium pump activity, increasing intracellular calcium in vascular smooth muscle and causing persistent vasoconstriction and hypertension.

## Practical Electrolyte Balancing Rules

1. **Aim for 4,700 mg Potassium Daily:** Consume potassium-dense whole foods (avocado, spinach, sweet potatoes, coconut water) daily.
2. **Salt Whole Foods Mindfully:** Use unrefined sea salt or iodized salt on nutrient-dense whole foods while avoiding processed packaged snacks containing hidden sodium.
3. **Ensure Adequate Magnesium:** Magnesium is a required co-factor for Na+/K+-ATPase pump stability. Without magnesium, cells cannot retain potassium effectively.
