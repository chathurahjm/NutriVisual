## The 3 PM Brain Fog Trap

Most professionals try to solve afternoon brain fog with another cup of coffee or a sugary snack. This creates a temporary insulin spike followed by a massive cognitive crash.

To maintain stable mental energy, your brain requires steady, clean fuel and active protection against oxidative stress. Feeding your neurons the right micronutrients is the key to unlocking peak focus.

## The Top 5 Cognitive Powerhouses

1. Wild Atlantic Salmon: Rich in long-chain Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) which build cell membranes in the brain and decrease cognitive decline.

2. Blueberries: High in anthocyanins, which cross the blood-brain barrier to increase cerebral blood flow and memory retention.

3. Matcha: Contains L-Theanine, which promotes alpha brain waves (relaxed focus) and works in synergy with natural caffeine to prevent jitters.

4. Raw Walnuts: The ultimate brain food containing polyphenols and alpha-linolenic acid (ALA) for cellular repair.

5. Dark Chocolate: High in cocoa flavanols that boost nitric oxide, relaxing blood vessels and delivering more oxygen to the brain.
