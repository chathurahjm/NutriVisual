## The Power of Polyphenols

Both dark chocolate (85%+ cacao) and wild blueberries are loaded with polyphenols—organic compounds that combat oxidative stress and prevent cell aging.

Blueberries contain high levels of anthocyanins, supporting cognitive processing and motor coordination. Dark chocolate contains epicatechin, which promotes vascular health and blood flow. Combining them is the ultimate biohacker dessert.
