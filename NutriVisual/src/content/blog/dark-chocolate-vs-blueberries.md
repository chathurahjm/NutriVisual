## The Power of Polyphenols

Both dark chocolate (85%+ cacao) and wild blueberries are celebrated for their dense concentration of **polyphenols**—organic compounds that combat oxidative stress and slow cellular aging. However, they target different pathways in the body.

Blueberries are packed with **anthocyanins**, the pigments that give them their dark color. Anthocyanins cross the blood-brain barrier to support memory and motor coordination. Dark chocolate is rich in **flavanols (epicatechins)**, which stimulate the endothelium to produce nitric oxide, relaxing blood vessels.

## Oxygen Radical Absorbance Capacity (ORAC)

| Metric | Wild Blueberries | Dark Chocolate (85%+) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ORAC Value** | High (~9,620) | Extremely High (~20,800) |
| **Key Polyphenol** | Anthocyanins | Cocoa Flavanols |
| **Vascular Impact** | Anti-inflammatory | Vasodilation (Nitric Oxide) |
| **Cognitive Benefit** | Neural communication | Cerebral blood flow |

Combining a square of dark chocolate with a handful of blueberries creates the ultimate brain-boosting, cardiovascular-protective dessert.
