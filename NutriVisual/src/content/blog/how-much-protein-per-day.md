## How Much Protein Do I Need Per Day?

The **Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA)** for protein is **0.8 grams per kilogram** of body weight per day for sedentary adults. However, this is a *minimum to prevent deficiency* — not an optimal amount for muscle maintenance, recovery, or longevity.

| Population Group | Protein Requirement | Example (70kg / 154lb person) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sedentary adults** | 0.8 g/kg/day | 56g protein/day |
| **Recreationally active** | 1.2–1.6 g/kg/day | 84–112g protein/day |
| **Strength athletes** | 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day | 112–154g protein/day |
| **Endurance athletes** | 1.2–1.8 g/kg/day | 84–126g protein/day |
| **Adults over 65** | 1.0–1.3 g/kg/day | 70–91g protein/day |
| **Pregnancy & lactation** | 1.1–1.5 g/kg/day | 77–105g protein/day |

## Is 100g of Protein a Day Too Much?

No — for most adults who exercise regularly, **100g of protein per day is well within healthy and beneficial range**. Research published in the *British Journal of Sports Medicine* shows that intakes up to 1.6 g/kg/day (112g for a 70kg person) maximize muscle protein synthesis rates.

Exceeding 2.2 g/kg/day shows diminishing returns for muscle building but is not harmful for healthy individuals with normal kidney function.

## What Happens If You Don't Eat Enough Protein?

Chronically low protein intake leads to:

- **Muscle loss (sarcopenia)** — especially after age 40, when muscle mass declines ~3–8% per decade
- **Weakened immune function** — antibodies are proteins
- **Slower wound healing and recovery**
- **Hair thinning and brittle nails**
- **Increased hunger** — protein is the most satiating macronutrient (protein leverage hypothesis)

## How to Distribute Protein Throughout the Day

Research from the *Journal of Nutrition* suggests that distributing protein evenly across 3–4 meals (25–40g per meal) stimulates muscle protein synthesis more effectively than consuming most protein in a single evening meal.

| Meal | Target | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Breakfast** | 25–30g | 3 eggs + Greek yogurt |
| **Lunch** | 30–40g | Grilled chicken breast salad |
| **Snack** | 15–20g | Cottage cheese + almonds |
| **Dinner** | 30–40g | Salmon fillet + lentils |
