## Understanding Glycemic Load

Simple carbohydrates (like donuts, white bread, and table sugar) are stripped of fiber. This allows your body to digest them almost instantly, causing a rapid spike in blood glucose.

In response, your pancreas dumps insulin to clear the sugar, leaving you feeling exhausted, irritable, and craving more carbs within an hour.

## The Fiber Buffer

Complex carbohydrates like Quinoa, Oats, and Sweet Potatoes are bound to insoluble and soluble fibers. This creates a gel-like matrix in your digestive tract, slow-releasing glucose into your bloodstream for hours of stable focus.
