## The Caffeine Rollercoaster

Coffee delivers a rapid spike of caffeine into the bloodstream, causing a quick increase in cortisol and adrenaline. While this boosts focus temporarily, it often triggers jitters and leads to a cognitive crash as adenosine receptors flood your brain.

Matcha tea contains a similar amount of caffeine but behaves completely differently due to a unique amino acid: L-Theanine.

## The L-Theanine Synergy

L-Theanine crosses the blood-brain barrier to promote alpha brainwave activity, which is associated with relaxed alertness and deep focus. It binds with caffeine to slow down its absorption rate, providing a smooth, sustained release of mental energy over 4 to 6 hours.

Additionally, matcha contains high levels of EGCG (epigallocatechin gallate), a powerful antioxidant that supports neuroprotection and metabolic health.
